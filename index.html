Established tafsir comes first.

Our study tools are designed to favor established tafsir and evidence over easy agreement. Their grounding rules instruct the AI to cite its sources, preserve scholarly differences, and distinguish its own reflections from commentary.

Draw on 18 tafsir editions, from al-Tabari and Ibn Kathir to al-Zamakhshari and Ibn ʿAshur, alongside 31 translations across 12 languages, word-by-word meanings, and detailed Arabic grammatical analysis. Sourced from quran.com and Quran Foundation.
